## Findings

- **MECO speed matches within ~2.5%** on every flight: the energy the stage delivers is right.
- **The sim reaches MECO ~10-15 s early and ahead on the curve.** Stage-1 burns its ascent propellant at full thrust (one throttle law, q-hold at 32 kPa), while Falcon 9 throttles down through max-q (real peak q 23-29 kPa vs the sim's 32 kPa target) and burns longer at lower acceleration. The sim's trajectory is therefore more aggressive in time, not in end state.
- **Booster timelines start right (boostback within ~2%)**; later events differ by up to ~20% because the sim flies its own target orbit, inclination and pad, not each mission's.
- Next model work: a Falcon-style throttle bucket around max-q and per-flight mission settings (inclination, target orbit) would tighten the ascent-curve error.
